From mirror to guide从镜子到向导

A therapeutic trajectory — the same mapping, learning to lead 一条疗愈轨迹 —— 同一个映射，学着去引导
Sound States began as a mirror→it read your state and reshaped sound to match it. In Relax and Sleep it now takes its first steps as a guide: it starts where you are and leads you toward calm. The vision is a guide you can trust→tested, fitted to you, and able to start from anywhere. Sound States 起点是一面镜子→读你的状态、重构声音去匹配它。现在在放松和睡眠里，它迈出了做向导的第一步：从你此刻的状态出发，把你带向平静。愿景是一位值得信任的向导→经过验证、贴合你本人、从任何起点都能出发。
An extension, not a spin-off. The same spine (state → sound), with one more ability (a trajectory). 递进，不是衍生。 同一条脊椎（状态 → 声音），多一个能力（轨迹）。
01 · THEORY SPINE01 · 理论脊梁

The ISO principle — a first version, not yet provenISO 原则 —— 做了第一版，还没被证明

Music therapy calls this the ISO principle: start with music that matches how the person feels (Altshuler, 1944), then move toward where they want to be. When I went to the experiments, the picture turned out more interesting. Matching first doesn't calm people down any better than going straight to calm music (Lowe-Brown et al., 2026). What does help is a guided sequence that ends somewhere calm — and in a five-night trial, people who started with matched music were far more likely to keep using it (90% vs 67% completion; Ding et al., 2026, preprint). So the match should be short, and the weight goes on guiding you down and letting you settle. Sound States now does a short version of both halves in Relax: a few seconds of matching, then a minute or two of guiding down to a calm landing, slowing down whenever your heart rate won't follow. Sleep's wind-down arc stretches the same way. What's missing is proof and fit: whether this guiding actually helps, how long each step should last for you, and a guide that works from any starting point, not just these two boards. And the subtlety: heart rate measures arousal, not intention — so where to guide is chosen by you, while heart rate only steers the music toward it. 音乐治疗里把这叫作 ISO 原则：先放贴合当事人情绪的音乐（Altshuler, 1944），再慢慢带向目标。我去查了实验，发现情况更有意思：先匹配并不比直接放平静音乐更能让人平静下来（Lowe-Brown 等, 2026）；真正有帮助的是有引导、并以平静收尾的顺序。而在一项连续五晚的试验里，从匹配开始的人更愿意坚持用下去（完成率 90% 对 67%；Ding 等, 2026，预印本）。所以匹配应该很短，重点放在把你带下来、并让你停在那里。现在放松板块已经做了两半的一个短版：先匹配几秒，再用一两分钟引导到平静落点，心率跟不上时就放慢。睡眠的入睡弧也同样会随心率伸缩。缺的是证据和贴合：这样引导到底有没有用、每一步对你来说该多长，以及一个从任何起点都能出发的引导，而不只是这两个板块。关键是：心率测的是唤醒，不是意图——所以「引导去哪」由你选，心率只在目标内引导。

Evidence: the Relax guide and the Sleep down-arc are built; what's missing is testing them and generalising to any starting point. 证据指向 放松的引导和睡眠下行弧都已建成；缺的是验证它们，并推广到「任意起点」。
02 · THE MECHANISM02 · 机制

How guidance works — generalize the arc引导怎么做 —— 推广那条弧

To lower arousal we already hold the levers: soften the attacks, thin the texture, slow down, and never let anything arrive suddenly. Sudden, loud or sharp sounds trigger the brain-stem reflex and wake you up (Juslin, 2013); softer attacks are heard as less energetic (Eerola et al., 2012). That is exactly the arc the Sleep board already runs. A second lever is breathing: slow, paced breathing at about six breaths a minute is the core of HRV biofeedback (Lehrer & Gevirtz, 2014). I'm trying it first in the physical piece, where you sit down and choose to follow; it isn't part of the website. Guidance = generalizing that one arc into 「any state → a calmer target」. 降唤醒的手段已经有了：让起音变软、让织体变稀、放慢、绝不让任何声音突然出现。突然、响亮、尖锐的声音会触发脑干反射，把人惊醒（Juslin, 2013）；起音更软的声音，听起来能量更低（Eerola 等, 2012）。这正是睡眠板块在跑的那条弧。第二个手段是呼吸：每分钟约 6 次的慢速节律呼吸是 HRV 生物反馈的核心（Lehrer & Gevirtz, 2014）。我先在实体作品里试：那里你会坐下来、主动去跟；它不放进网站。引导 = 把这条弧推广成「任意状态 → 更平静目标」。

Mood-regulation strategies (Saarikallio & Erkkilä, 2007): Discharge · Diversion · Solace · Revival — letting it out, getting away, being comforted, resting to recover. v1 already lets you pick one of these in the guide zone; the vision is to carry you along it. Guiding toward a target is not new either — MPTrain (Oliver & Flores-Mangas, 2006) chose songs to steer runners toward a target heart rate; what is new here is doing it for the calm states, by changing structure. 情绪调节策略（Saarikallio & Erkkilä, 2007）：宣泄 · 转移 · 慰藉 · 休息恢复。v1 已经让你在引导区里自己选一种；愿景是陪你沿着它走下去。「朝目标引导」本身也不新——MPTrain（Oliver & Flores-Mangas, 2006）就用选歌把跑步者带向目标心率；这里新的是把它带到平静的状态，而且改变的是结构。
03 · SENSOR ROADMAP03 · 传感器路线图

Read richer, not just louder读得更丰富，不是更大声

Today a $3 PPG reads heart rate reliably. The next honest step is HRV — the same optical signal's richer readout, the standard marker of stress vs. calm. Farther out: EDA (skin conductance) and consumer EEG — high uncertainty, placed at the far edge. For now all of this runs on the paired phone — sensor in the ear, brain in your pocket; fully on-bud, no phone, is the far-future version. 现在 $3 PPG 可靠读心率；下一步是 HRV —— 同一光学信号更丰富的读数，压力 / 平静的标准指标；再远是 EDA 皮电 + 消费级 EEG 脑电 —— 高不确定性，放最远端。现阶段这些都跑在配对手机上——传感器在耳、大脑在口袋；全塞耳机、不要手机，是远期版本。

04 · HONEST BOUNDARY04 · 诚实边界

A design hypothesis, not a therapy设计假设，不是治疗

Music affects people differently — the same sound soothes one person and irritates another. I claim no therapeutic effect; the vision is a testable direction, not a cure. 音乐因人而异 —— 同样的声音安抚一个人、烦扰另一个人。不声称 疗效；愿景是可验证的方向，不是治病。
