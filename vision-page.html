From mirror to guide从镜子到向导

A therapeutic trajectory — the same mapping, learning to lead 一条疗愈轨迹 —— 同一个映射，学着去引导
Sound States v1 is a mirror→it reads your state and reshapes sound to match it. The vision is a guide→the same mapping, but it walks you from where you are toward where you want to be. Sound States v1 是一面镜子→读你的状态、重构声音去匹配它。愿景是一位向导→同一个映射，但从「你在哪」走向「你想去哪」。
An extension, not a spin-off. The same spine (state → sound), with one more ability (a trajectory). 递进，不是衍生。 同一条脊椎（状态 → 声音），多一个能力（轨迹）。
01 · THEORY SPINE01 · 理论脊梁

The ISO principle — half built, half missingISO 原则 —— 做了一半，缺了一半

Music therapy's ISO principle (Altshuler, 1948): begin where the person is, then move the music toward the target state. Sound States already does the first half — high arousal routes to energetic sound, matching the listener. Missing is the second half: a trajectory that carries them down. And the subtlety: heart rate measures arousal, not intention — so where to guide is chosen by you, while heart rate only steers the music toward it. 音乐治疗的 ISO 原则（Altshuler, 1948）：先匹配当事人当下状态，再把音乐导向目标。现在做了前一半——高唤醒 → 能量音乐 = 匹配；缺后一半——把唤醒往下带走的轨迹。关键是：心率测的是唤醒，不是意图——所以「引导去哪」由你选，心率只在目标内引导。

Evidence: the fig-1 sleep down-arc — the mechanism that carries you down is already built; what's missing is generalizing it from "sleep only" to any starting point. 证据指向 fig-1 睡眠下行弧 —— 「带下去」的机制已经建成，只差从「睡眠专用」推广到「任意起点」。
02 · THE MECHANISM02 · 机制

How guidance works — generalize the arc引导怎么做 —— 推广那条弧

To lower arousal we already hold the levers (Juslin's brain-stem reflex + rhythmic entrainment): slow down, soften, thin the texture, remove the pulse — the exact arc the Sleep board runs. Guidance = generalizing that one arc into 「any state → a calmer target」. 降唤醒的杠杆已有（Juslin 脑干反射 + 节奏夹带）：变慢、变软、变稀、去脉冲 —— 正是睡眠板块在跑的那条弧。引导 = 把这条弧推广成「任意状态 → 更平静目标」。

Mood-regulation strategies (Saarikallio & Erkkilä, 2007; Saarikallio, 2008): Discharge · Diversion · Solace · Revival — letting it out, getting away, being comforted, resting to recover. v1 already lets you pick one of these in the guide zone; the vision is to carry you along it. Guiding toward a target is not new either — MPTrain (Oliver & Flores-Mangas, 2006) chose songs to steer runners toward a target heart rate; what is new here is doing it for the calm states, by changing structure. 情绪调节策略（Saarikallio & Erkkilä, 2007；Saarikallio, 2008）：宣泄 · 转移 · 慰藉 · 休息恢复。v1 已经让你在引导区里自己选一种；愿景是陪你沿着它走下去。「朝目标引导」本身也不新——MPTrain（Oliver & Flores-Mangas, 2006）就用选歌把跑步者带向目标心率；这里新的是把它带到平静的状态，而且改变的是结构。
03 · SENSOR ROADMAP03 · 传感器路线图

Read richer, not just louder读得更丰富，不是更大声

Today a $3 PPG reads heart rate reliably. The next honest step is HRV — the same optical signal's richer readout, the standard marker of stress vs. calm. Farther out: EDA (skin conductance) and consumer EEG — high uncertainty, placed at the far edge. For now all of this runs on the paired phone — sensor in the ear, brain in your pocket; fully on-bud, no phone, is the far-future version. 现在 $3 PPG 可靠读心率；下一步是 HRV —— 同一光学信号更丰富的读数，压力 / 平静的标准指标；再远是 EDA 皮电 + 消费级 EEG 脑电 —— 高不确定性，放最远端。现阶段这些都跑在配对手机上——传感器在耳、大脑在口袋；全塞耳机、不要手机，是远期版本。

04 · HONEST BOUNDARY04 · 诚实边界

A design hypothesis, not a therapy设计假设，不是治疗

Music affects people differently — the same sound soothes one person and irritates another. I claim no therapeutic effect; the vision is a testable direction, not a cure. 音乐因人而异 —— 同样的声音安抚一个人、烦扰另一个人。不声称 疗效；愿景是可验证的方向，不是治病。
