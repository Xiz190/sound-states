How to listen怎么听
Start with A · Full track and drag heart rate from high to low — the sound gets duller and darker, but every instrument stays.
Then switch to B · Stems and do the same — the strings and bass really leave, until solo piano carries it. That emptiness is something the full track can’t do.
What changes is not the volume but the make-up — which layers are playing.先放 整首 A，把心率从高拖到低——声音变闷、变暗，但四件乐器一直都在。
再切 分轨 B，同样从高拖到低——弦乐、贝司会真的退场，只剩钢琴独奏垫底。那个"空"是整首版做不出来的。
改的不是音量，是构成（哪些层在响）。
